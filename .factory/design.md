# Visual thesis — glacial minimal ceramics

Podcast Recall Loop should feel like placing three small porcelain memory pieces on a quiet worktable. The cool palette lowers the pressure around review. Rounded, slightly uneven edges make each prompt feel made by the learner rather than generated by a system.

## Tokens

- Ice field `#F2F6F4`: page background; a cold matte glaze.
- Porcelain `#FCFDFC`: raised surfaces.
- Ink `#172722`: primary text (13.8:1 on ice).
- Slate `#52625D`: secondary text (5.8:1 on ice).
- Glacial blue `#176B78`: actions and focus (5.5:1 on ice).
- Deep teal `#0D4F59`: pressed actions.
- Moss `#456A48`: remembered/success.
- Ochre `#8A5814`: due/warning.
- Clay red `#9B3E35`: errors and destructive actions.
- Night kiln `#101B1A`, night porcelain `#182624`, frost text `#EDF5F2`: dark treatment.

## Type and spacing

The display face uses Georgia, a locally available serif stack, for reflective questions. The body uses the system sans stack for fast scanning and small download size. No remote fonts load. The type scale is 14, 16, 18, 24, 38, and 58px. Spacing follows 4/8px steps, with a 72px section rhythm on desktop and 48px on phones. Text measures stay below 70 characters.

## Shape and layout

Prompt sheets use 22–32px asymmetric radii and thin blue-grey rims, like hand-finished ceramic slabs. The hero is left-led, with a still-life occupying the right half. Short ruled marks resemble timestamp ticks. The application workspace keeps the podcast context close to the recall question and avoids dashboard charts.

## Interaction and motion

The signature motion is a 220ms “set down” transition: a new prompt moves 8px upward while its shadow settles. Buttons compress by 1px. Route changes fade in over 160ms. No animation loops. With `prefers-reduced-motion`, all transforms and smooth scrolling are removed and state changes are immediate.

## Responsive treatment

At 390px, the still-life becomes a shallow band beneath the first action. Secondary explanations collapse. Forms and review controls stack. Tap targets remain at least 44px. Bottom safe-area padding protects installed-app controls.
